Finance Manager
Harbor Goods Co. (a consumer products distributor with 400 employees)

About the role
You will run financial planning and reporting for the company and lead a team of three analysts. You will report to the Chief Financial Officer.

Must have
- 5 or more years in finance, accounting or financial planning and analysis, with at least 2 years managing people.
- The monthly close and management reporting: profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flow.
- Budgeting and forecasting, including building and maintaining a driver-based financial model.
- Variance analysis, explained clearly to non-finance managers.
- Advanced Excel; experience with an ERP system such as NetSuite or SAP.
- A bachelor's degree in finance, accounting or economics.

Nice to have
- A CPA, CMA or ACCA qualification, or an MBA.
- Experience with a business intelligence tool such as Power BI or Tableau.
- Experience improving or automating finance processes.

What you will do
- Own the annual budget and the quarterly forecast.
- Lead the monthly close with the accounting team and present the results.
- Build analyses that support pricing, purchasing and hiring decisions.
- Manage and develop the analyst team.
