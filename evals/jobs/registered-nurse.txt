Registered Nurse, Medical-Surgical Unit
Lakemont General Hospital (a 320-bed community teaching hospital)

About the role
You will care for adult patients on a 32-bed medical-surgical unit, working twelve-hour shifts with a team of nurses, nursing assistants and physicians. You will assess patients, give medications and treatments, and teach patients and families before they go home.

Must have
- An active Registered Nurse (RN) license.
- 2 or more years of acute care nursing experience, ideally on a medical-surgical unit.
- Patient assessment and care planning for 4 to 6 adult patients per shift.
- Safe medication administration, including IV therapy and IV pump management.
- Charting in an electronic health record (EHR) such as Epic or Cerner.
- Current Basic Life Support (BLS) and Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS) certification.
- Patient and family education, including discharge teaching.
- A Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN), or an associate degree in nursing with a BSN completed within 3 years of hire.

Nice to have
- Medical-surgical nursing certification (CMSRN).
- Telemetry or step-down experience.
- Experience as a preceptor for new nurses.

What you will do
- Assess, plan and deliver care for an assigned group of patients each shift.
- Give medications and IV therapy, and watch for and report changes in condition.
- Chart care in Epic and hand over clearly at shift change.
- Teach patients and families and prepare them for discharge.
