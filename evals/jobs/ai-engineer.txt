Applied AI Engineer
Northwind Labs (a technology company building a web platform for small businesses)

About the role
You will build the AI features inside our product: agents and assistants that read documents, call tools and take work off our customers' hands. You will work directly with customers and internal teams to turn a business process into a working, measured AI workflow, and you will own it from the first prototype to production.

Must have
- 3 or more years building software in production, with at least 1 year building applications on large language models.
- LLM application work: prompt and tool design, structured output, agents or multi-step workflows.
- Evaluation of model behaviour: test sets, evals or regression checks you built and used.
- Python or TypeScript, written daily, with a web framework for APIs.
- A relational database (PostgreSQL or similar) and experience turning unstructured data into structured data.
- Cloud infrastructure experience.
- A bachelor's degree in computer science, information systems or a related field.

Nice to have
- A master's degree in a related field.
- Retrieval-augmented generation, fine-tuning or self-hosted models.
- Customer-facing delivery: gathering requirements and handing over a finished workflow.
- Public work samples, such as a code repository.

What you will do
- Design and ship LLM agents and document workflows for real business processes.
- Build the evaluations that show a workflow is correct before and after each change.
- Work with customers to scope, deliver and measure each workflow.
