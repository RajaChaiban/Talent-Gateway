Account Executive, Mid-Market
Quillstack (a software company selling scheduling and workforce software to businesses with 100 to 1,000 employees)

About the role
You will sell Quillstack to mid-market companies, running the full sales cycle from the first meeting to the signed contract. A sales development representative books meetings for you, and you will report to the Head of Sales.

Must have
- 2 or more years as a quota-carrying account executive selling B2B software (SaaS).
- A record of meeting or beating an annual new-business quota of $600,000 or more.
- Running the full sales cycle: discovery, product demos, proposals, negotiation and closing.
- Building your own pipeline through outbound prospecting as well as inbound leads.
- Keeping the pipeline and your forecast accurate in a CRM such as Salesforce or HubSpot.
- Selling to several decision makers in one deal, including finance and operations leaders.
- A bachelor's degree in business or a related field.

Nice to have
- A sales method such as MEDDICC or the Challenger Sale.
- Selling HR, scheduling or workforce software.
- Working with a sales engineer on technical evaluations.

What you will do
- Run discovery calls and product demos for new prospects.
- Build and close a pipeline of mid-market deals each quarter.
- Keep Salesforce up to date and forecast your quarter accurately.
- Work with customer success to hand each new customer over smoothly.
