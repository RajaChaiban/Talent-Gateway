Digital Marketing Manager
Fieldstone Outdoor Co. (an online retailer of camping and hiking gear with 150 employees)

About the role
You will lead the company's digital marketing: paid search and social, email and the website's search ranking. You will own a monthly budget, report results to the Head of Growth and manage two marketing specialists.

Must have
- 4 or more years in digital marketing, with at least 1 year managing people or agencies.
- Paid search and paid social campaigns (Google Ads, Meta Ads): planning, running and optimising them.
- Managing a paid media budget of $50,000 a month or more against a return target (return on ad spend or cost per acquisition).
- Email marketing: segmentation, automated flows and A/B testing in a tool such as Klaviyo or HubSpot.
- Search engine optimisation (SEO): keyword research, on-page changes and reporting.
- Analytics: Google Analytics 4, and reports that tie spend to revenue.
- A bachelor's degree in marketing, business, communications or a related field.

Nice to have
- E-commerce experience, ideally on Shopify.
- Experience with a marketing automation or customer data platform.
- Basic SQL or spreadsheet modelling.

What you will do
- Plan and run campaigns across search, social and email.
- Own the monthly budget and report spend, revenue and return each week.
- Test landing pages, offers and messages, and keep what works.
- Manage and develop the two marketing specialists.
